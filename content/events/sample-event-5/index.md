Wellnergy Festival is the UK's leading festival of wellness, bringing together thousands of people for an inspiring day dedicated to health, happiness, and personal wellbeing. Set in the heart of London, the festival features world-class speakers, fitness experiences, live entertainment, mindful workshops, and interactive activities designed to help you feel your best.

Looking after your mental wellbeing is just as important as your physical health, which is why our team will be there throughout the festival. Visit our wellbeing space whenever you need a moment to pause, reset, or simply have a chat. Whether you're feeling overwhelmed, need a quiet space, or just want to connect with someone who understands, we're here to help you make the most of your Wellnergy experience.
