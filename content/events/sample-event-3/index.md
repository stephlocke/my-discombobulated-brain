Found Festival is an intimate, 2,000-capacity boutique gathering set in the stunning Buckinghamshire countryside. Over the August Bank Holiday weekend, the festival will host over 100 incredible folk, roots, and indie acts across three stages—including This Is The Kit, Stornoway, and The Felice Brothers—alongside non-stop dance and community celebrations.

Because great music goes hand-in-hand with a healthy mind, our team will be on-site all weekend. We are creating a dedicated, safe sanctuary to help you pause, reset, and chat about mental health whenever the festival energy gets a bit too loud. Come enjoy the music, find your community, and let us look after your wellbeing!
