Laura’s work with NCMH is very personal and varied, but we would like to talk about a few collaborations. Laura went to Time to Talk London on 5th February 2015 with Prof Ian Jones. She is a prominent Research Champion for them, promoting their DNA database and research.

Laura was honoured to be the first guest on the first NCMH podcast and collaborates with them and Tilly Ellis, one of our trustees, annually with the Express Yourself art show, which happens on University Mental Health Day.

We also collaborated with NCMH and photographer Alex Stewart on 28th February 2018 on a Photographic Exhibition, ‘Why don’t you smile.’
